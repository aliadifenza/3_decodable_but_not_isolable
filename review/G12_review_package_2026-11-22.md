## 1. The original plan (authoritative; read this first)

**Seed (pre-protocol).** Purely Kantian: *"a relational circuit (Causality) cannot function without drawing from an upstream entity circuit (Substance)."* Setup: small model (Gemma-2-2B), prompt like "The heavy bowling ball rolled into the fragile glass vase, causing the vase to [shatter]". Ablate the upstream Substance features (the entity token features), leave the downstream untouched, measure the log-odds of the correct token. **Falsified** if Substance ablation hurts no more than (C1) an equal-magnitude set of unrelated features, or no more than (C2) ablating a grammatically present but causally irrelevant bystander noun. **Confirmed** if Substance ablation hurts significantly more than both.

**Protocol V1.1 (PI-approved 2026-11-20; binding).** Philosophical question widened: *Can Plato's Forms and Kant's conditions of possibility explain and predict the organization of neural-network representations and their downstream circuits?* Core hypothesis: networks learn organizing structures (interpreted as Forms) that permit recognition across different manifestations of a phenomenon. **"A Form is not necessarily a perfect exemplar or a single feature. It may be a multidimensional, distributed structure."** Specific hypothesis: Substance → Causality — *the internal representation establishing the identity of an entity is a necessary upstream component of a downstream circuit processing its causal interactions.*
- **P1 Form/invariance:** representation stable across manifestations of an entity, distinct from other entities (property-matched non-members, noun-free descriptions, grammatical roles, leave-one-role-out). Added at G2 so that "Substance" is not merely the noun token or its material.
- **P2 Causal dependency:** D = L_intact − L_intervened (target log-odds); Δ1 = D_Substance − D_C1, Δ2 = D_Substance − D_C2; both must pass.
- **P3 Mandatory pathway:** pathway-specific disruption + restoration.

**G5 spec freeze (2026-11-21).** Layer, feature-set size and strength frozen as *selection rules* on discovery/validation only. Substance operationalized as a greedy set of **≤ 8 SAE latents** (Gemma Scope 16k, L0 ≈ 70) at the **last token of the reference**, layers 2–14. A layer is valid only if R1 (efficacy: removing ≥ 67% of identity information downstream, amnesic AUC ≤ 0.70, role-probe drop ≤ 0.05), R2 (identity validity), R3 (controls) and P3 feasibility all pass. No valid layer ⇒ P2/P3 inconclusive; nothing re-tuned. Evaluation set sealed until G10.

**Fidelity note we want you to check.** The frozen ≤ 8-sparse-latent, single-position operationalization is narrower than V1.1's "multidimensional, distributed" Form. We consider this a design narrowing introduced at G5 (drafted by Claude, approved by PI), not a change of hypothesis. Do you agree?

## 4. Exploratory follow-up E1–E3 (labelled exploratory; PI-approved 2026-11-21 19:38; never feeds P2/P3)

Purpose: test three mundane explanations of the G5r null. Code `exploratory/e_probe.py` (tests `test_e_local.py`: 7 PASS, incl. exact replication of G5r's k ≤ 8 latent order and k = 8 R1 metrics); analysis `exploratory/e_analyze.py`; raw results `exploratory/results/e_results_layers_*.json`; summary `e_summary.md/json`; figure `fig_e1e3.png`. Two Modal runs, 78 GPU-min, ≈ $1.43. Discovery + validation only.

- **E1 Dose:** ablate k ∈ {8, 16, 32, 64, 128} identity latents (first 8 = G5r order; continued greedily, same worst-stratum AUC criterion, from top-512 pool).
- **E2 Place:** at the last token of the reference ("last", = G5r) vs at **all** tokens of the reference span ("all"; mean ≈ 10 tokens for descriptions). Crossed with E1 and E3.
- **E3 Whole:** erase one linear identity direction from the **full residual** (includes the SAE reconstruction error), fit per kind on discovery site representations: (a) mean-difference orthogonal projection; (b) LEACE for a binary concept with 10% covariance shrinkage.
- **Controls:** same-k random latents that are active on discovery members (freq ≥ 0.10), disjoint from the identity set; a covariance-matched random direction (Xc'g) erased the same way.
- **Measures (= R1, on validation):** downstream removal r = 1 − (AUC_ablated − 0.5)/(AUC_clean − 0.5) with a probe trained on clean discovery at layer L+4, final token; same-site amnesic AUC (fresh probe trained on ablated discovery, tested on ablated validation); role-probe AUC drop; ‖Δ‖/‖h‖; next-token KL; 1,000-sample stratified item bootstrap CIs. The frozen R1 bar is used only descriptively.
- **Optional D comparison:** for conditions meeting the R1 bar on a patient kind: D on eligible discovery causal items; C1 = the condition's random control at the patient, C2 = the same method fitted for the bystander, applied to the bystander. **No magnitude matching (deviation from Decision 2).**

## 6. Draft claims (please rate each)

1. **[HIGH]** Gemma-2-2B represents entity identity (kind vs property-matched look-alikes, across descriptions and roles) strongly and linearly at the reference position, layers 2–14.
2. **[HIGH]** Under the frozen rules, no layer qualified; P2 (Substance → Causality log-odds dependency) and P3 (mandatory pathway) are **inconclusive** — not supported, not refuted. The evaluation set remains sealed.
3. **[HIGH, exploratory]** The G5r null is not a dose or placement problem: ablating up to 128 identity-selected SAE latents, at one or all reference tokens, removes ≈ 0 downstream identity; random active latents with ~5× larger edits also remove ≈ 0.
4. **[MED, exploratory]** Erasing a single linear identity direction from the full residual (including SAE error) makes identity linearly unrecoverable at the site but removes only ~15–35% (median) of identity read downstream; a matched random direction removes ≈ 0. ⇒ identity is carried by the representation in a way that sparse SAE latents miss and that the model largely reconstitutes downstream.
5. **[LOW, exploratory]** This pattern is *consistent with* the PI's description of identity as an organized, distributed whole ("organized togetherness; no single part essential") and with V1.1's "multidimensional, distributed" Form — but it is **not evidence for** it (see §7).
6. **[HIGH]** No conclusion about Substance → Causality is possible from the exploratory D comparison (≤ 5 items, effects ≤ 0.1 log-odds).

## 7. Known weaknesses — please test these and add others

a. **Amnesic AUC = 0.50 is by construction for E3, not a finding.** After either erasure the class means of the fitting data are equal in every direction; for a convex L2-logistic probe the optimum is then w = 0, so the fresh probe outputs a constant. It shows no *linear* identity remains *in the fitting data's mean structure*; it says nothing about non-linear identity or about a probe trained on validation. Is claim 4's "unrecoverable at the site" therefore overstated?
b. **Information may already have moved before layer L.** All interventions edit only reference-span positions at layer L. Attention in layers < L can already have copied identity into the final token and other context tokens, which the downstream probe then reads. This alone could explain the "reconstitution" in claim 4 without any "organized whole". The decisive test (not run) is erasure at all positions or at the read position as well. How much does this undermine claims 4–5?
c. **r is a ratio with a small denominator.** Downstream clean AUC is 0.70–0.76 at L2 and L5–L8. The L6 peak (r = 0.55) and the cases meeting the bar sit where the denominator is small; their CIs are very wide (upper bounds > 1).
d. **LEACE and mean-difference share the removed direction** (cosine 1.00 in every case; binary-concept LEACE always edits along Σ_xz ∝ mean difference) and differ only in the read-out vector. Why does mean-difference remove more? Is the 10% shrinkage the cause?
e. **Identity latents barely fire:** k = 128 identity latents change the site only ~6% vs ~30% for random active latents. Is E1 a fair dose test, or did the selection criterion (worst-stratum AUC) pick rare latents?
f. **Data:** validation/evaluation are Claude-authored from templates; discovery causal items have low baseline eligibility. Does this threaten claims 1–4?
g. **Selection effects:** layers 2–14 all reported; nothing chosen post hoc. Exploratory analyses were run after seeing G5r. Is the exploratory label sufficient, or must anything be removed?
h. **Overall:** is the honest headline "identity is present but not removable by sparse SAE ablation; linear erasure removes it locally but it largely returns downstream; the Kantian dependency remains untested"? Suggest a better one if not.
